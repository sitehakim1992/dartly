1 Overview

🎉 Dartly does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app works completely offline.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Dartly mobile application ("the App") available on the Google Play Store. By using Dartly, you agree to this policy.

Dartly is a Dart programming language learning app offering lessons, quizzes, and exercises. All content is bundled within the app. No internet connection is required.

2 Data We Collect

We collect zero personal information. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, device ID, or any other personal identifier.

Dartly does not include analytics, crash reporting, advertising SDKs, or any network-connected services that would transmit data from your device.

3 Local Storage

To provide a personalized learning experience, Dartly stores the following information locally on your device only, using AsyncStorage:

  • Completed lesson IDs
  • Quiz scores per lesson
  • Total XP points earned
  • Daily learning streak count
  • Bookmarked lesson IDs
  • Your chosen theme (dark/light)

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to any server. You can delete all stored data at any time from the Profile screen → "Reset All Progress".

Uninstalling the app will automatically remove all locally stored data.

4 Third-Party Services

Dartly does not use any of the following:

  • Advertising networks or ad SDKs
  • Analytics platforms (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel)
  • Crash reporting services (e.g., Crashlytics, Sentry)
  • Social login providers
  • Payment processors
  • Cloud storage or sync services

The app makes no network requests of any kind during normal usage.

5 Children's Privacy

Dartly is suitable for users of all ages, including children under 13 (COPPA) and under 16 (GDPR). Since the app collects no personal data and has no network connectivity, there is no risk of children's data being collected or shared.

Parents and guardians can feel confident that Dartly is a safe educational application.
