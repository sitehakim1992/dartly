## 🇬🇧 ENGLISH

### Short Description (80 chars max)
Learn Dart 3.0 with 60 lessons, quizzes, exercises & offline support. Free!

### Long Description
Master the Dart programming language from absolute beginner to advanced expert — completely offline, at your own pace.

📚 60 Structured Lessons
Carefully crafted lessons covering Dart 3.0+ from Hello World to Clean Architecture, organized into three levels:
• 🌱 Beginner (20 lessons) — Variables, types, functions, loops, collections, OOP basics
• ⚡ Intermediate (20 lessons) — Async/await, streams, generics, mixins, null safety
• 🔥 Advanced (20 lessons) — Isolates, metaprogramming, design patterns, clean architecture

🧠 Interactive Quizzes
Each lesson includes 3 quiz questions with instant feedback and score tracking. Challenge yourself and reinforce what you learn.

✏️ Hands-On Exercises
Every lesson comes with a coding exercise with starter code, your own editor, and a reference solution. Learn by doing.

💻 Modern Code Viewer
Syntax-highlighted Dart code blocks with copy button, output preview, and a dark terminal aesthetic.

🎮 Gamified Learning
Earn XP points, level up from "Dart Newbie" to "Dart Master", maintain daily streaks, unlock achievements, and track your progress across all 60 lessons.

📴 100% Offline
All 60 lessons are bundled inside the app. No internet connection needed. Learn anywhere, anytime — on a plane, in the subway, or in a remote area.

🌙 Dark & Light Themes
Beautiful dark mode by default, with a clean light mode toggle. Gradient-rich design that makes learning feel premium.

🔖 Bookmarks & Search
Save any lesson for later and search through all content instantly by keyword, topic, or level.

Why Dart?
Dart is the language behind Flutter — the world's most popular cross-platform UI framework used by millions of developers. Learning Dart opens doors to building mobile, web, and desktop apps with Flutter.

Whether you're a complete beginner or an experienced developer learning Dart for Flutter, Dartly gives you everything you need — structured, offline, and free.

### Launch Strategy (Week 1–4)

Week 1 — Soft Launch
- Publish on Google Play (open testing)
- Submit to 3 Flutter/Dart Facebook groups
- Post on r/FlutterDev, r/dartlang (Reddit)
- Post on X/Twitter with #flutter #dart #programming

Week 2 — Community Push
- Create a short demo video (screen record walkthrough, 60 sec)
- Post on LinkedIn targeting Flutter & mobile developers
- Submit to Product Hunt
- Post on Dev.to: "I built a 60-lesson Dart learning app — here's what I learned"
- Share in Flutter Discord servers

Week 3 — Content Marketing
- Write "Top 5 Dart features every Flutter dev should know" on Medium, link to app
- Post YouTube Shorts: "Did you know Dart can do this? (Lesson 32 preview)"
- Respond to Dart questions on Stack Overflow with app mention in bio

Week 4 — Outreach
- Email Flutter newsletter curators (Flutter Weekly, Flutter Digest)
- Reach out to 5 Flutter YouTubers for a review
- Create a free landing page (dartly.app) with screenshots and Play Store link

---

### Ongoing Growth
- Monthly content updates: add new lessons, tips, or challenges
- Localization: app is already described in Arabic, French, Spanish — localize the UI too
- Google Play Experiments: A/B test icon and feature graphic
- Ratings prompt: after completing 5 lessons, show an in-app rating request
- Share feature: "I just completed Lesson X on Dartly!" shareable card
- Referral: "Share with a friend" generates a deep link to the app

### Target Audience
1. Developers learning Flutter who need Dart fundamentals
2. Computer science students (Arabic-speaking markets especially)
3. Self-taught developers in Africa, Middle East, Latin America (offline-first markets)
