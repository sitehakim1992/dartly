1 Overview

🎉 Dartly respects your privacy. We do not collect or store any personal data directly on our servers.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Dartly mobile application ("the App"). Dartly is an interactive learning platform for the Dart programming language, offering tutorials, exercises, and quizzes.

To support ongoing development and keep all educational content free, Dartly integrates third-party mobile advertising networks including Google AdMob and Start.io.

2 Information We & Third Parties Collect

Direct Data Collection by Us: ZERO

We do not directly collect, request, or store your name, email address, phone number, account credentials, or contact lists on any external backend server.

Data Collected Automatically by Third-Party Ad Services (AdMob & Start.io):

When you use the app, third-party advertising SDKs may automatically collect non-personally identifiable technical information to serve relevant ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID), Android ID, or other persistent software identifiers.
  • Device Technical Data: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, system language, and screen dimensions.
  • Network & General Location Data: IP address, mobile network status, country/city-level general location derived from IP, and network connection type.
  • Ad Interaction & App Diagnostics: Ad views, clicks, impressions, crash logs, diagnostic performance data, and app interaction events.

3 Local Data Storage

To track your learning progress offline and deliver a personalized experience, Dartly saves user state information strictly on your local device using local storage mechanism (AsyncStorage):

  • Completed lesson & topic IDs
  • Quiz scores and percentage completion
  • Total XP points earned
  • Daily learning streak metrics
  • Bookmarked lesson IDs
  • App theme preferences (Dark/Light mode)

This data never leaves your device. You can reset or wipe this local data anytime via Profile → "Reset All Progress" or by uninstalling the app.

4 Third-Party Advertising Services (AdMob & Start.io)

Dartly uses trusted third-party advertising partners to display advertisements (Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded ads). These partners may use device identifiers and technical data to serve contextual or personalized advertisements.

For more details on how our ad partners collect and process data, please review their privacy policies:

  • 👉 Google AdMob & Privacy Terms
  • 👉 Start.io Privacy Policy

Managing & Opting Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising identifiers on your device at any time by navigating to Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization.

5 Children's Privacy

Dartly is designed for general audiences interested in learning programming. We do not knowingly collect or request personal information from children under 13 (COPPA) or under 16 (GDPR).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided data through third-party ad networks in our app, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps immediately.

6 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in app features, ad networks, or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
